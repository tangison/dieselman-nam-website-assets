# DESIGN

## Concept

**Industrial motion across the Namib.**

The design combines two real visual systems already present in the business:

1. Heavy mechanical detail: engines, diagnostics, undercarriages, steel, grease, tools.
2. Long-distance movement: trucks, rail lines, asphalt, desert, horizon, and directional perspective.

## Signature navigation

- A floating graphite navigation rail.
- The supplied transparent logo sits inside a CSS-created circular medallion.
- The medallion may overlap the rail to resolve the logo’s circular silhouette naturally.
- The menu trigger has two lines only.
- Orange appears as a substantial active field, not a collection of tiny badges.
- Desktop and mobile preserve the same logic instead of becoming unrelated navigation systems.

## Home composition

1. Rail-line or road hero with a large orange structural block.
2. Oversized service statement with short, direct contact action.
3. Service selector using a horizontal technical track.
4. Alternating mechanical close-ups and wide transport imagery.
5. Owner-led About moment using Stephen’s real portrait.
6. Work gallery as a swipeable horizontal image rail.
7. Contact close with phone and WhatsApp prioritised.

## Composition constraints

- Never place all photographs in equal cards.
- Documentary client imagery carries proof. Generated supporting imagery carries atmosphere and explanation only.
- Do not place the logo over every image.
- Do not obscure important machinery with body copy.
- Keep essential text out of high-detail image zones.
- Alternate dense mechanical texture with calmer colour fields.
- Use orange at large scale at least once per major route.
- Do not repeat the exact same split layout across consecutive sections.
