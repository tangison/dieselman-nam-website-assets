## Generated-image disclosure

The restored files were produced through image editing from supplied real client frames. They are not raw documentary originals. The untouched evidence remains in `assets/source/`.

Do not use the restored files as evidence for a mechanical result that the source frame did not show. Do not use them for before-and-after claims.

The ten `support-*` files are original generated imagery created for this website pack. They are not stock or Unsplash images. Use them as supporting atmosphere or service explanation only. Never caption them as Dieselman Nam staff, vehicles, premises, customers, or completed work.
